Practice mode

Try the workflow with a sample patient. No fingerprint or face scan takes place.

A little guidance

What happens next?

  1. 01
    Your request is checked

    The incident number, reason, and confirmation must be complete.

  2. 02
    Identify with an approved device

    Follow the connected scanner's instructions to check the patient's identity.

  3. 03
    Emergency details open

    Review allergies, medicines, conditions, and emergency contacts. Verify details before acting.

Every access leaves a record

This helps protect the patient and makes emergency access accountable.

How is patient privacy protected?

Only emergency information is released. A real scanner keeps raw fingerprints and face images outside this page. Access is tied to the responder, incident, reason, and time.

Does the patient have a QR card?

Scan it with your phone camera to open their enabled emergency summary. You do not need this form.
