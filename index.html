Security controls

What protects the patient?

  1. Verified responderStaff accounts are provisioned by an administrator, not self-selected.
  2. Liveness + 1:N matchA certified service checks a live sample against enrolled templates.
  3. Replay protectionEvery scan is bound to a short-lived, single-use server nonce.
  4. Minimum disclosureOnly emergency fields return; the patient registry is never searchable here.
  5. Immutable audit trailWho, why, when, incident, scanner, and match transaction are recorded server-side.
Demo mode simulates the external scanner and labels the audit method accordingly. It is not real biometric identification.
